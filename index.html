Farmer Advisory Corner

Scientific, practical practices to protect your crop investment, maintain soil fertility, and handle agricultural chemicals safely.

Soil Testing Importance

Test soil every 2-3 years. Identifying accurate N-P-K deficiency, pH levels, and electrical conductivity prevents overspending on unneeded chemical fertilizers.

  • Collect V-shaped 6" samples
  • Test before Kharif & Rabi

Certified Seed Storage

Moisture is the biggest enemy of seed viability. Keep certified hybrid packets elevated on wooden pallets in dry, well-ventilated farm sheds away from direct damp walls.

  • Store at cool ambient humidity
  • Keep original seed lot tags

Drip & Micro-Irrigation

Conserve up to 50% water with inline drip systems and root-zone fertigation. Periodically flush venturi injectors and screen disc filters to avoid salt scaling.

  • Flush end-caps weekly
  • Acid treatment for clogged drippers

Safe Chemical Spraying

Always use full PPE (protective mask, eye goggles, rubber gloves). Check technical triangle warnings on labels and never spray against headwind directions.

  • Dispose empty containers safely
  • Spray in early morning or evening
